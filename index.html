GAMIQ — free random mini-games that measure your gaming skill

GAMIQ is a free browser game where you play short mini-games one after another, in random order, and get a score for your gaming skill. You never know which game comes next — so instead of practicing one game, GAMIQ shows how well you adapt.

There are 30 mini-games in 5 genres (REFLEX, SPEED, AIM, LOGIC and MEMORY). Each game takes about 20–40 seconds. It works on PC and smartphone, in English and Japanese, with no download and no sign-up.

▶ Play GAMIQ

How to play

  1. Press PLAY. A random mini-game starts — read the one-line rule and play.
  2. When it ends you get a score from 0 to 100. Swipe up (or scroll) for the next game.
  3. The more you play, the more accurate your GAMIQ score becomes. Your level goes up and the games get harder.

FAQ

Is GAMIQ free?

Yes. GAMIQ is completely free to play in your browser. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Can I choose which game to play?

No. Games come in random order on purpose. GAMIQ measures how well you handle games you did not prepare for.

What is the GAMIQ score?

A number from GAMIQ's own scoring of your mini-game results across REFLEX, SPEED, AIM, LOGIC, MEMORY and ADAPT (how well you adapt to new games). It is a game score, not a medical or psychological IQ test.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. GAMIQ works on smartphones (swipe for the next game) and on PC (scroll or use the keyboard).

What is the Daily Challenge?

Once a day, everyone plays the same 5 games — one from each genre — and the results go on a daily ranking.
