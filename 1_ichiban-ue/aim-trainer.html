What does it measure?

Precision (how close to the center you hit), control (not hitting the wrong target) and timing (acting at the right moment). Each game is scored from 0 to 100.

Why random games?

Aim trainers usually repeat one drill. In GAMIQ you never know what comes next, so you see how your aim holds up on games you did not prepare for — and how it compares with your other skills.

Tips for better aim

  • Aim first, then click: accuracy turns into speed.
  • Keep your wrist and arm relaxed.
  • On PC, a slightly lower mouse sensitivity often helps.
  • Short daily sessions beat one long session.
